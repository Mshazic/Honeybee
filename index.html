More than honey

Pollinators help plants produce fruit and seeds

As pollinators move between flowers in search of nectar and pollen, they can transfer pollen that plants use to produce fruit or seed.

Make space for bees

  • Plant native flowering species suited to your region.
  • Choose diverse plants that bloom across the growing season.
  • Limit or eliminate pesticide use where possible.
